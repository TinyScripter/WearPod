# Privacy Policy for AntennaPod Fork

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026  
**Application ID:** kleinabersmart.antennapod  

## Overview
This application is an open-source fork of the AntennaPod podcast player, modified solely to provide support for Wear OS devices. This app is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. We do not track users, do not collect personal data, and do not operate proprietary backend servers.

---

## 1. Data Collection and Tracking
* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, location, contacts, or device identifiers).
* **No Analytics or Third-Party Trackers:** This app contains no analytics libraries, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

---

## 2. Network Activity
The application connects to the Internet exclusively to fulfill user-requested features:
* **Podcast Feeds & Media:** When subscribing to podcasts or streaming/downloading episodes, the app connects directly to the servers operated by the respective podcast publishers.
* **Podcast Search:** Podcast discovery requests are sent directly to public podcast directories (such as the iTunes Search API or Podcast Index) as initiated by the user.

---

## 3. Storage and Permissions
* **Local Storage:** Downloaded podcasts, playback state, subscriptions, and settings are stored locally on your device.
* **Permissions:** All requested Android and Wear OS permissions (e.g., Internet access, foreground service for playback, notifications) are strictly used to enable core app functions.

---

## 4. Open Source
This project is open-source software and is based on the original [AntennaPod project](https://antennapod.org).

---

## 5. Contact and Inquiries
If you have any questions, bug reports, or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue in the public repository:
